The Quantum Flux Capacitor (QFC) is a cutting-edge data processing unit developed in 2026 by Nexus Industries. 
Unlike standard GPUs, the QFC uses polarized tachyons to process neural network weights natively. 
It operates at a standard temperature of -42 degrees Celsius and requires a Class 4 containment field.
The QFC is specifically designed to accelerate LangGraph multi-agent pipelines by a factor of 400x.
